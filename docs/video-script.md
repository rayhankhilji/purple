# PURPLE: 3-minute video script

Version 3 · 25 October 2026 · for the `/submit` video (MP4 or MOV, 3:00 at most). Record from https://purple-wine.vercel.app in Chrome,
full screen, 1920×1080, zoom 100%. Keep the Mubit console logged in on a second tab (Tasks → Concept designer of 2026).

Rules for every word on screen and in the voice-over: never say CFD, wind tunnel or validated; our lap times are a model prediction
from our physics simulator; real lap times come from OpenF1; scores are n/N; say that the Silverstone replay runs inside our older game.

The site follows the same four steps as this script: 1 Mubit engineer, 2 Which AI is best?, 3 vs no AI, 4 Race & drive.

## Running order

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Homepage (step 1), replay paused on the first decision | "A Formula 1 team only gets a limited number of expensive tests. Deciding what to test next is the job. PURPLE is an AI engineer for that job." |
| 0:12 | Press Play at 4×. The car changes shape, PRPL climbs the board | "The model suggests each change through Mubit. Our rules check and physics simulator test it on a lap of Silverstone. The car only keeps what is legal and faster." |
| 0:32 | Point at a green and a red card in the Mubit feed | "Every card is one Mubit call. Red means physics said no, and that verdict goes back into the next call. Green means physics said yes, and only those become Mubit training examples." |
| 0:50 | Exam tab | "We tested this on 30 car situations the engineer had never seen. With physics corrections and a shared memory of past approved decisions, Haiku went from 9 out of 30 good ideas to 23." |
| 1:10 | Step 2, Model GP. Let the replay run, then the podium | "Because every call goes through Mubit, any model can sit in the same seat. Same prompt, same memory, same 150 simulations each. GPT-6 Luna won, Sonnet came second. Opus and Haiku lost to the no-AI control, and we show that." |
| 1:40 | Press Race them | "Here are their best cars on one clock, with the real 2026 pole lap." |
| 1:55 | Step 3, vs no AI | "On a cheap simulator, brute force does well too, and we report it. Real tests are expensive. There, every idea has to count, explain itself and take corrections." |
| 2:15 | Mubit console: the task, its examples, the model versions | "Mubit trained a first small model on our early calls. It scored 5 out of 19 on Mubit's own test, so Mubit kept the big model answering. Since then we have sent it over 4,000 physics-approved examples, plus a shorter answer format a small model can match exactly." |
| 2:35 | Step 4, Race & drive: press Drive it | "And you can drive the best car yourself, against the real lap. Our model predicts 87.7 seconds against a real pole of 88.1." |
| 2:50 | Close card: purple-wine.vercel.app | "An F1 engineer that learns from physics, through Mubit. The Silverstone track runs in our older game; everything else was built this weekend." |

## If something goes wrong

- The replay is slow to start: press Skip to end and talk over the finished run.
- The race page is slow to load: skip it and end on the Model GP podium.
- If Mubit has trained a new model before you record, show its score on the console at 2:15 and say the number as n/N.
