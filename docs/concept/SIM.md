# CONCEPT lap simulator (Silverstone)

Every output carries this label: **"physics lap sim · quasi-steady-state point mass · Silverstone OSM centreline · calibrated to 2026 British GP qualifying"**

This is the deterministic benchmark the car-design optimiser calls. It computes one flying qualifying lap (time, sectors, speed and gear/rpm trace, energy use) from a car's physical numbers: mass, aero coefficients per active-aero mode, PU power and the 2026 energy rules, tyres, brakes and gearing. **Speeds come out of the physics and are never fitted as targets.** Telemetry is used for two things only:

1. Estimating parameters nobody publishes, on **one** lap: the pole lap's speed trace.
2. A **held-out check** on 10 other teams' laps.

Per-team car parameters are inferred from public lap times and speed traps. They are not team data.

Code is in `concept/sim/` (plain JS for Node and the browser). Tests: `test/concept-sim.test.js`. Overlay figures: `docs/concept/sim-trace-{ant,had,sai}.svg`.

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `track.js` | Track geometry, cached: the racing line, curvature, grade, FIA Straight Mode zones and timing lines. It reads `js/data/silverstone.js` read-only, in an isolated `vm` context with a `window` shim (no `document`). |
| `line-silverstone.js` | GENERATED by `build-line.js` from `concept/data/telemetry/`. Holds the racing-line path, the alignment to the OSM frame, the timing lines, the corner markers, the pole lap's reference speed trace and the gearing estimate. |
| `lapsim.js` | `simulateLap`, `baselineCar`, `priorCar`, the regulation constants (REGS), model assumptions (MODEL), default conditions |
| `fit.js` | `calibrate()` (parameter estimate on the pole lap), `fitTeams()` (competitor model), `heldOut()`, `teamCar()` |
| `fitted.js` | GENERATED by `build-fit.js`: the baseline estimate, the 11 team cars and their fit and held-out residuals |
| `index.js` | Public API (`require("./concept/sim/index.js")`; in the browser load `js/data/silverstone.js`, then `concept/sim/{track,line-silverstone,lapsim,fitted,fit,index}.js` to get `window.EI.conceptSim`) |
| `build-line.js`, `build-fit.js`, `plot-trace.js` | Node scripts that regenerate the line, the fit and the SVG overlays |

## 1. Contract

```js
simulateLap(car /* PhysicalCar */, "silverstone", conditions?, opts?) →
{ lap_s, sectors_s: [s1, s2, s3], vmax_kmh, speed_trap_kmh, vmin_kmh,
  energy: { deployed_mj, harvested_mj, harvested_braking_mj, harvested_superclip_mj, recharge_cap_mj,
            superclip_share, superclip_fill, energy_limited, strategy, deploy_ceiling_kmh },
  speeds_kmh: { intermediate_1, intermediate_2, finish_line }, air_density_kgm3, label,
  trace? /* opts.trace */: { s_m[], v_kmh[], aero_mode[], gear[]?, rpm[]? } }   // s_m: metres from the control line
baselineCar(teamId?)   // no id: the estimated generic 2026 car; "redbull" etc.: that team's inferred car (demo team hook)
calibrate(opts?), fitTeams(opts?), heldOut(fit, dir), teamCar(id), teamCars(), cornerTable(car?), fitted(), LABEL
```

- **`conditions`:** `{ air_temp_c, track_temp_c, air_density_kgm3?, air_pressure_hpa?, humidity_pct?, wind_ms, wind_from_deg, wet, fuel_kg?, recharge_limit_mj? }`. The defaults are the 2026 British GP qualifying session (§4.4).
- **`opts`:** `trace`, `strategy` (`"threshold"` is the default; `"marginal"` and `"optimal"` are alternatives, §3), `overtake` (the C5.2.8 ii curve), `no_superclip`, `line` (`"telemetry"` is the default, `"osm"` is the alternative), `cal` (stays at identity: §5 has no correction factors).
- **Additive PhysicalCar fields, read defensively:**
  - `aero.floor_stalled` (or a top-level `floor_stalled`) gives CLA × 0.5.
  - `stall_margin_mm` is informational.
  - `gearing: { rpm_per_kmh[8], shift_rpm }` produces the gear and rpm trace and the C5.2.4 power limit below 10,500 rpm.
  - CLA is taken as already evaluated at `aero.ride_height_mm` (by the design layer), so `ride_sens_per_mm` is informational.
  - `cog_height_m` is height above ground and feeds lateral load transfer.
  - `session` is not used. Use `conditions.recharge_limit_mj`.
- **Determinism:** fixed iteration counts, Float64 throughout, no randomness. The sim makes no network calls and no Mubit calls, and never touches `data/` or `data-mock/`.

## 2. Track

- **Racing line (default):** the **mean path of the 11 team qualifying laps** (OpenF1 X/Y, `concept/data/telemetry`). Positions are averaged at equal lap fractions, then mapped into the OSM frame of `js/data/silverstone.js`.
  - The mapping is a Procrustes similarity fit: reflection, a 1.38° rotation and 5.3 m RMS against the OSM centreline.
  - Averaging matters. One lap alone shows position glitches, for example a 22° heading jump in 25 m before Copse.
  - The path is then Gaussian-smoothed with σ equal to one position-sample interval of travel: max(6 m, v·0.26 s). The OpenF1 location feed is about 3.9 Hz. The reference speed is used only to set that spacing.
  - Curvature is the heading change per metre, smoothed with σ = 4 m.
  - Only the path comes from the laps. Speeds are never taken from them.
- **Alternative:** `opts.line = "osm"` uses a minimum-peak-curvature line (iteratively reweighted, multiscale projected gradient) inside the 15 m OSM corridor. It is ~1.8 s slower because its fast-corner radii are tight.
- **Frame and timing:** s is the distance along the pole lap from the control line (5824.6 m; FIA centreline 5891 m).
  - The control line sits at OSM s = 80.4 m, not at the game's `startFinish` 260 m.
  - Sector lines are 1807.5 m and 4239.1 m, the points where the pole lap's own time channel reaches S1 and S1+S2.
  - The speed trap is at 4780 m: the telemetry README's fit of 11 laps' trap readings, RMS 0.8 km/h. The FIA text says "140 m before T15", which would be 4840 m.
  - Intermediates are at 1840 m (110 m before T6) and 4170 m (50 m after T14).
- **Straight Mode zones:** the FIA activation points A1 (65 m after T18), A2 (55 m after T5), A3 (155 m after T7) and A4 (65 m after T14), taken from the corner markers. Deactivation points are not published, so each zone runs to the next corner (T1, T6, T9, T15).
- **Elevation:** from the OSM data (12 m relief), mapped through the alignment.

## 3. Model

The car is a point mass with m = mass + fuel and m_e = 1.03 m. Air speed is u = v + headwind, where the headwind is the component along the heading (same convention as `lab/aero.js`), and q = ½ρu².

1. **Aero:**
   - Axle loads are F_zf = m g w_f + q·CLA_f and F_zr = m g (1 − w_f) + q·CLA_r.
   - Straight Mode is used only inside an FIA zone, after a ≤ 400 ms switch (the car runs in Corner Mode during the switch). It is also closed when lateral demand exceeds 50 % of Straight Mode grip, and Corner Mode always applies under braking.
   - Straight Mode coefficients are capped at the Corner Mode values. The flaps only lower incidence, so Straight Mode can never add load or drag without losing downforce.
2. **Tyres:** μ(F_z) = μ₀(1 + ls − ls·F_z/F_z0) per tyre. Lateral load transfer is ΔF = m_axle·a_y·h/track, and the axle capacity is solved in closed form. a_y,max is the smaller of the front and rear axle values. The friction ellipse couples a_x and a_y. Traction is rear-only; braking uses both axles, capped by `max_decel_g_mech`.
3. **Resistance:** drag ½ρu²·CDA, rolling 0.012·(m g + aero load) and grade.
4. **Power:** P_wheel = η_dt·(P_ICE(N) − P_clip/0.97) + η_dt·0.97·P_K.
   - P_K is at most min(350 kW, C5.2.8 taper).
   - P_ICE(N) is reduced below 10,500 rpm by (0.27N + 165)/3000 (C5.2.4).
   - The K deploys only what traction can use.
5. **Passes:**
   - Apex limits come from a_y,max by bisection.
   - The backward braking pass runs in Corner Mode.
   - The forward traction pass starts from the slowest apex. Braking harvest is min(350 kW, 0.97·η·0.43·F_brake·v).
6. **Energy per lap:**
   - Deployed ≤ 4 MJ state-of-charge window + harvested.
   - Harvested ≤ min(car cap, conditions limit, 8.5 MJ). Silverstone qualifying is 6.5 MJ.
   - Deployment between those totals is not tracked within the lap.
7. **Strategy (default `"threshold"`):**
   - The K deploys below a speed threshold v\*, and superclips (harvests at full throttle) above it at a uniform power share.
   - The share fills a fraction φ of what braking leaves of the recharge cap. φ ∈ [0, 1] is chosen for the fastest lap by a 5-point golden-section search, so superclipping is optional.
   - v\* is solved by bisection so the energy budget closes.
   - This reproduces the speed plateau and fade seen in the real qualifying traces.
   - `"marginal"` orders deploy and superclip by lap-time sensitivity (Σ ds/v³ to the next braking point, soft-reset by deceleration). `"optimal"` is the cap-ordered equal-marginal rule. Both are available for study but are not the calibrated default.
8. **Gearing:** the lowest gear under `shift_rpm` (8 gears, C9.6.1). It is used for the rpm trace and the C5.2.4 limit. Shift times are not modelled.

Smoothness matters to the optimiser. Lap time responds to a 0.25 % CLA step by about 0.025 s, which is the physical slope, with ±0.005 s noise. The speed trap can step by up to ~3 km/h between nearby designs because φ comes from a discrete search. The two fixes made along the way are a soft reset of the sensitivity sum, and choosing φ by search instead of a fixed point that had two stable solutions 0.1 s apart.

## 5. Estimation on one lap (no correction factors)

**Method:** a maximum a posteriori estimate (Nelder–Mead, 320 iterations, deterministic) of **4 global physical parameters**: tyre μ scale, CLA scale (both modes), CDA scale (both modes) and load sensitivity. Each has a log-normal prior around the prior car (σ 0.15 / 0.3 / 0.3 / 0.7). The data is **ANT's 88.111 s pole lap only**. The objective is the speed RMS on its 5 m grid plus (5 km/h per s)² × lap-time error².

- No per-corner factors are used, and no global "grip" or "drag" fudge is applied on top: `global = {1, 1}`.
- The priors stop weakly identified directions from running away. Without them the fit pushed CLA to ~10 m² and traded it against rolling load.

**Fit-lap result:** the sim lap is 88.309 s against the real 88.111 s (+0.20 s); the trap is 309.0 against 302 km/h. Speed RMS is **13.1 km/h**. Sectors are 28.048 / 36.359 / 23.902 against 28.348 / 35.521 / 24.242.

## 7. Limitations (biggest first)

1. **Mid-speed braking corners:** the sim is 20–40 km/h too slow at Stowe, Becketts and Brooklands, and it is S2-slow and S3/S1-fast on every lap. A single μ(F_z) and CLA cannot give 5 g at 230 km/h and 2.5 g at 90 km/h at once. Missing pieces: transient platform effects, kerb use, the line's mid-corner shape (smoothed), and the fact that real drivers trail-brake. The lap time is still matched because these errors largely cancel.
2. **Energy strategy:** threshold deploy plus optional uniform superclip is a behavioural model fitted only through the 4 parameters. The state-of-charge trajectory within the lap and the 50 kW/s power-reduction rate limit are not modelled. Real peaks are higher and real fades longer. Recharge allowance up to 6.5 MJ is monotone to within ~0.02 s; φ is chosen by a 5-point search.
3. **Effective parameters:** the estimated CLA (5.6 m²) and CDA (1.35 m²) are effective values of this point-mass model fitted on one lap. They are not wind-on-car measurements, and design-layer coefficients (`concept/physical.js`) are on a different scale until calibrated against this baseline.
4. **Team cars:** driver, session (Q1 against Q3), tow and strategy are all folded into the car numbers. Treat them as a plausible field, not as copies of team cars.
5. **Point mass:** no yaw or transient dynamics, no longitudinal load transfer, ideal brake balance, no shift time, no tyre temperature or wear. Wind is a headwind component only. Wet is a single μ factor.
6. **Geometry:** a single averaged racing line is used for every car and every design.

## 8. Timing and regeneration

- One lap takes ~6–22 ms CPU in Node 22 after warm-up (about 90 forward passes for the energy strategy). The test requires < 30 ms CPU (best of 5 batches).
- The first call builds the track in ~1 s, then it is cached.
- `node concept/sim/build-line.js` rebuilds the line from `concept/data/telemetry`. `node concept/sim/build-fit.js` re-estimates, refits and re-checks in ~25 s; it is byte-deterministic, and `--check` exits non-zero if `fitted.js` is stale. `node concept/sim/plot-trace.js ANT HAD SAI` redraws the overlays.
- Telemetry comes from OpenF1 (openf1.org), an independent community project not associated with Formula 1, the FIA or FOM. It is used here for non-commercial research.
